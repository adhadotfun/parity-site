Parity Yield

Deposit into an ERC‑4626 vault on Robinhood Chain 4663, hold a tradeable share, redeem any block: no lockup, no queue, no epoch. Every number on this page is read live from the chain, and every transaction is signed by your own wallet. What the vaults are still missing is a fee source, and the panel below counts exactly how many of them are waiting for one.

Where the fee goes

A hook earns the fee.
Something has to hold it.

Parity is a Uniswap v4 hook: it prices corporate actions so the pool keeps the value instead of donating it to the first arber. That surcharge is income, and income needs somewhere to sit. The place already exists on the same chain: a plain ERC-4626 vault whose share price rises as fees arrive. Deposit the asset, hold a tradeable share, redeem any block.

The socket is empty
Reading chain 4663…

Every vault on this chain exposes a harvester() address: the one account allowed to push fee income in and lift the share price. Read them live and the number on the left is what comes back.

That empty slot is the right default. A yield wrapper over tokenized equities is only safe once something upstream survives a split, a dividend, a rebase. Push raw swap fees from an unguarded equity pool into a vault and you are not compounding yield, you are compounding the leak: the arber takes the corporate action, the LP eats it, and the vault dutifully reports a higher share price on a pool that just lost more than it earned.

This is why the hook comes first. Parity prices the corporate action so the fee is worth harvesting; the vault is where that fee lands once it is. Wiring the second to the first before the first exists is how you build a machine that compounds a loss.

Stated plainly

What this tool does not fix.

Read these before you sign anything. They are the reasons not to.

01
No vault here earns anything yet.

The panel above is a real ERC-4626 deposit against live contracts, and it works. But a vault only earns once a fee source is wired to its harvester, and right now every tokenized-equity vault on 4663 returns the zero address. Depositing today buys you a share priced at exactly one, redeemable for exactly one. That is the honest state of it.

02
These vaults are unaudited.

They are live contracts on a young chain, and no audit covers them. The panel reads them and calls deposit and redeem from your own address, never ours: approving a token is a real approval, and a deposit is a real transfer. Every vault links to Blockscout, and you should check the address before you sign.

03
The zap crosses a live pool, so the price moves.

Paying in USDG means a real swap on a real Uniswap v3 pool before the deposit happens. The quote you see is simulated against current liquidity and submitted with 1% slippage tolerance; on a thin pool a large order will fill worse than a small one, and the shown minimum is what protects you, not the estimate. Every fee tier is quoted and the best one is used, but the best available fill can still be a bad fill.
